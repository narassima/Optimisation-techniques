Linear Programming Problem (LPP)Analytical technique to optimize (determine the best solution) based on specific objective(s) subject to constraintsReal-world relevance: Structured approach to complex problems in resource allocation, scheduling, portfolio building, and decision-making with large solution spaces Key AssumptionsLinearity: Relationships between variables are linear Deterministic: Parameters are known and constant Additivity: Total effect is the sum of individual contributions Types of solutionAny solution that satisfies the constraints is called feasible solutionA feasible solution that maximizes or minimizes Z is called optimal solution
